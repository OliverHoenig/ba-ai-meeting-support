\chapter*{Note on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}

AI applications were used to edit the language of this paper. The content, argumentation,
and academic evaluation were developed independently by the author.

AI applications were used to assist with research in the preparation of this paper. The
academic review, selection, and classification of the content were carried out by the author.

AI-based programming tools were used to assist in the implementation of the prototype. This
was done in consultation with the supervising examiners.

Since English is not the author’s native language, DeepL \cite{DeepLAIPlatform} was used in some places to
translate sections of German text written by the author into English.
