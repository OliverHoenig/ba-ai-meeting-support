\chapter{Theoretical Foundations}
This chapter presents the theoretical foundations and the current state of research. These form the basis for
the subsequent work. Based on a systematic literature review, five key challenges in the context of meetings
were identified (\ref{sec:key-challenges-in-meetings}). These serve as the starting point for the in-depth
analysis in the following expert interviews (see Chapter \ref{ch:empirical-analysis-of-facilitating-strategies}) and
provide indicators of what to focus on during the subsequent requirements gathering process (see Chapter
\ref{sec:requirements-analysis}).
