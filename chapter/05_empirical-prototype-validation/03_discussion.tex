Äußerungen mit einem einheitlichen Muster: erst dem Vorredner danken, dann paraphrasieren. Dieses Muster

Psotiv anzumerken ist, das integieren von teilnehmdende wurde gernell als postiiv wahr genommen. Da dies die

nützlich ist."

The design already took into account the risk of talking too much, and the aging and cooldown mechanisms were
intended to prevent the repetition of the same thought. In the current prototype, any human interaction can
theoretically lead directly to another one. Based on the new conversation element, a new thought is
generated, which can theoretically be scored highly again and then brought up for discussion. Although this
thought is enriched by the speakers with the current meeting context, there is no evaluation of whether the
contribution is still meaningful. One approach could be that the system is re-evaluating the plant occurence,
before the thought is voiced, whether the contribution is still relevant.

The sessions show both facilitative effects and a predominant experience of disruption.
In isolated moments the agent introduced a perspective the group had not taken up, helped to
distribute speaking time more evenly, or drew attention to a point that would otherwise have
dropped out of the discussion.
Nearly all participants nevertheless experienced the agent as disruptive, or judged that it
took the floor too often.
These small positive effects on the meeting were outweighed by that cost.
Whether proactive facilitation is worthwhile beyond this design remains open.
The sessions identify a specific set of challenges in this prototype, and a smaller set of
potentials that the same meetings made visible.
